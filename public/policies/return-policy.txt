ShopAI Return Policy

General Return Window
Most products purchased from ShopAI can be returned within 7 days of delivery, provided they are unused, undamaged, and returned in their original packaging with all accessories included.

Category-Specific Rules
- Headphones & Earbuds: eligible for return only if the hygiene seal on the ear tips/cushions is unbroken.
- Laptops: return window extended to 15 days due to higher value. A 5% restocking fee applies if the original box has been opened.
- Smartphones: return window is 10 days. The device must not show any signs of physical or liquid damage, and must be returned with the original box, charger, and invoice.
- Smartwatches: 7-day return window; the return is void if the strap has been swapped for a non-original one.
- Monitors, Keyboards, Mice: standard 7-day window; physical damage to ports or keys voids eligibility.
- Cameras: 7-day return window; the shutter count and included lens must match the original shipment.

Non-Returnable Items
- Products marked "Final Sale" at checkout.
- Any item missing its original packaging, manuals, or accessories.

Refund Process
Once a returned item passes inspection, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method. Store credit refunds (if chosen) are issued instantly.

How to Initiate a Return
Go to "My Orders", select the item, choose "Return Item", and follow the prompts to schedule a pickup or drop-off.
