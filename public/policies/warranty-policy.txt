ShopAI Warranty Policy

Standard Warranty Coverage
- Laptops & Smartphones: 1-year manufacturer warranty covering hardware defects and manufacturing faults.
- Headphones, Earbuds & Smartwatches: 6-month warranty covering electronic and build defects.
- Monitors, Keyboards & Mice: 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects.
- Cameras: 1-year warranty on the camera body; lenses carry a separate 6-month warranty unless stated otherwise.

Battery Coverage
Batteries in any product are treated as a consumable component and are covered for 6 months only, regardless of the product's overall warranty period.

Extended Warranty
Extended warranty plans (1 or 2 additional years) can be purchased at checkout or within 30 days of delivery for an additional fee. Extended plans cover the same terms as the standard warranty.

What Voids the Warranty
- Physical damage (drops, cracks, dents).
- Liquid or water damage not covered under an explicit "water-resistant" claim.
- Unauthorized repairs or modifications by non-ShopAI-certified technicians.
- Removal or tampering with the warranty seal/serial number.

Claiming a Warranty Repair
Warranty claims are handled separately from returns and can be initiated at any time within the applicable warranty period via "My Orders" > "Request Warranty Service". Approved claims are repaired or replaced within 10-14 business days.
