ShopAI Shipping Policy

Delivery Timelines
- Standard delivery: 3-5 business days for most pin codes.
- Express delivery: 1-2 business days, available at checkout for an additional fee in serviceable areas.
- Remote or non-serviceable areas may take up to 7-9 business days.

Shipping Charges
- Orders above ₹999 ship free via standard delivery.
- Orders below ₹999 incur a flat ₹79 shipping fee.
- Express delivery is charged separately regardless of order value.

Order Tracking
Once shipped, a tracking link is sent via email and SMS. Live tracking is also available under "My Orders" > "Track Package".

Packaging
All products are shipped in tamper-evident packaging. Laptops, monitors, and cameras include additional protective cushioning. Please record an unboxing video if you suspect damage during transit, as it is required for damage claims.

Delayed or Lost Shipments
If a shipment is delayed beyond the estimated delivery window by more than 2 days, or shows no tracking movement for 48 hours, contact support for a replacement or refund. Lost shipments confirmed by the courier partner are fully refunded or replaced at no extra cost.

International Shipping
ShopAI currently ships only within India. International shipping is not available at this time.
